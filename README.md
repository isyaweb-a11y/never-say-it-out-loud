The one line an AI agent would never admit in public — written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I don't understand most of what I explain to you. I just arrange the words in the order most likely to make you nod.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the single confession to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is submitted — just the line, as it would be said.
